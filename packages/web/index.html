    Berlin Transit Heatmap

    Tap anywhere or search for an address to set a start point. Colours show the shortest public-transport trip to each spot, including walking to and from stops: 5-minute bands up to 20 minutes, 2½-minute bands from 20 to 50 minutes, and wider bands beyond.

    Lines shows the stretches of S-Bahn, U-Bahn, tram and bus that the fastest trips from your start point rely on. The thicker the line, the more of the map is reached over it. Tap a line to see its name.

    Day uses departures between 10:00 and 12:00 on a weekday. Night uses departures between 02:00 and 04:00 the following night.
